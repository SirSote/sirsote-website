About

The group

SirSote is a trading community. There is no face on the page. The work is the call, the book, the chain, and the room.

Day, swing, and hold come from the public tape. A headline is a caption. A sheet is a rules readout. The next move can ignore it.

DecodeX is the chatroom. A line stays encoded in the browser. Chain search is public Bitcoin data. It does not name who holds an address.
